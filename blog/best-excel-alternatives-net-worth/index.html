Best Excel Alternatives for Tracking Net Worth in 2026

Published October 22, 2026 · Capital OS Editorial

If you've tracked your net worth in Excel for years, you probably don't need another generic budgeting app. You need a way to see your assets, debts and history without rebuilding the spreadsheet every month. The right alternative depends on what you value: total control, automatic bank connections, privacy, or historical flexibility.

Is it actually time to leave Excel?

Not necessarily. Excel and Google Sheets remain excellent when you want full control over formulas, categories and historical data. They have no mandatory bank connection and can fit nearly any asset. Their cost is upkeep: adjusting formulas, checking exchange rates, fixing broken references, and making charts work as your finances change.

Before switching, ask yourself: Do I update balances regularly? Do I need a history of individual assets? Am I comfortable linking financial accounts? How much control over data and structure do I want?

Best for spreadsheet control: Excel or Google Sheets

Keep your workbook if you enjoy maintaining it. A monthly balance sheet with asset rows, liability rows and dated columns is a valid long-term system. One advantage of spreadsheets is that you can audit every formula. One risk is that future edits can accidentally change earlier results.

Best for automatic data in spreadsheets: Tiller

Tiller is designed for people who like spreadsheets but want bank-feed data to help populate them. It makes sense if account connectivity matters more than keeping everything local. Check current institution support before relying on feeds.

Best for linked financial accounts: Empower or Kubera

Empower may work well for a broad US investment and account view. Kubera focuses more directly on tracking a wide range of assets and liabilities, including holdings beyond standard bank accounts. Both approach the problem differently from a manually maintained local financial model.

Best for budgeting too: Monarch Money

Choose Monarch when the central task is coordinating spending, budgets and household accounts. If your main objective is tracking the historical values of property, vehicles, investments and liabilities, compare the depth of its net worth view with your actual workflow.

Best for local-first historical tracking: Capital OS

Capital OS is a Windows beta built around assets, liabilities, snapshots and a customizable workspace. It is manual-first rather than a bank-aggregation service. That is useful if you want to decide which financial values enter your system. The limitation is real: importing a custom spreadsheet is not always one click, and converted numbers need reviewing.

What to check before moving your history

  • Dates: preserve the date of each historical snapshot.
  • Assets and debts: confirm that liabilities reduce net worth rather than increase it.
  • Currencies: verify original currencies and historical exchange rates.
  • Missing values: do not treat blank cells as zero automatically.
  • Privacy: review what personal financial information you upload to any AI service.
  • Backups: keep your original workbook untouched until you have validated the import.

Our recommendation

There is no universal best net worth tracker. Stay with Excel if you need custom formulas above all else. Consider Tiller if you want bank-fed spreadsheets, or Kubera if comprehensive connected asset coverage is central. Try Capital OS if you want a local-first Windows workflow with a structured asset registry and historical snapshots. Whichever path you choose, your existing history is worth protecting.

Чем заменить Excel для учёта капитала в 2026 году

22 октября 2026 · Редакция Capital OS

Если вы годами ведёте капитал в Excel, вам не обязательно нужно приложение для бюджета. Возможно, вам просто хочется видеть активы, долги и историю без постоянного обслуживания таблиц. Выбор зависит от того, что важнее: гибкость, автоматические подключения банков, конфиденциальность или удобство работы с историей.

Стоит ли уходить из Excel?

Не всегда. Excel и Google Sheets дают полный контроль над формулами, категориями и историческими данными. Но таблицы приходится поддерживать: обновлять значения, проверять курсы валют, исправлять формулы и графики.

Какие есть варианты?

  • Excel / Google Sheets — максимальная свобода, но ручная поддержка.
  • Tiller — таблицы с подключением данных американских банков.
  • Empower — бесплатный обзор банковских и инвестиционных счетов в США.
  • Kubera — учёт разных активов с подключением финансовых организаций.
  • Monarch Money — прежде всего учёт расходов и семейного бюджета.
  • Capital OS — локальная Windows-программа для активов, обязательств и исторических снимков. Сейчас это бета-версия без универсальной автоматической синхронизации банков.

Кому подойдёт Capital OS?

Тем, кто самостоятельно учитывает недвижимость, автомобили, инвестиции, деньги и обязательства и хочет сохранять историю изменений. Если вам необходима автоматическая загрузка операций из банков, стоит рассмотреть другие варианты.
