Free Net Worth Excel Template: Track Assets, Debts and Your Progress

October 22, 2026 · Capital OS Editorial

You don't need a paid app to answer one useful question: are you actually getting wealthier? A net worth spreadsheet can be enough. Record what you own, subtract what you owe, and repeat on a schedule you can maintain.

What your tracker needs

Keep it boring: one row for each bank account, investment account, property or vehicle, and one row for each loan or other debt. Use a consistent reporting currency. Your net worth is total assets minus total liabilities.

Example: what the numbers mean

If your accounts and property total $300,000 and your debts total $180,000, your net worth is $120,000. That does not mean you have $120,000 available to spend: some of that value may be tied up in a home or retirement account.

How to build your own simple tracker

  1. List your assets, one item per row.
  2. List debts separately, with positive balances.
  3. Calculate net worth as assets minus debts.
  4. Keep a monthly or quarterly history instead of overwriting previous totals.
  5. Check that currencies, property estimates and loan balances are consistent.

Excel or Capital OS?

Excel wins for custom formulas and a free-form layout. Capital OS is a Windows beta built for local-first asset records and dated historical snapshots. It does not currently offer universal automatic bank syncing. You can compare workflows here, or read our step-by-step Excel tutorial.

There's no reason to change a spreadsheet that already works for you. But if updates, history, and multiple kinds of assets have turned into a chore, it's worth testing a structured alternative.

Что должно быть в таблице

Отдельная строка для счёта, брокера, машины, квартиры и каждого кредита. Затем итог: чистый капитал = активы − обязательства. Не смешивайте валюты без пересчёта.

Простой пример

Активов на $300 000, долгов на $180 000 — чистый капитал $120 000. Но это не значит, что вся сумма лежит на карте: часть денег может быть в недвижимости и инвестициях.

Как сделать простой учёт самостоятельно

  1. Запишите каждый актив отдельной строкой.
  2. Отдельно добавьте долги положительными числами.
  3. Вычтите долги из суммы активов.
  4. Сохраняйте итоги по месяцам, не стирая прошлые периоды.
  5. Проверяйте валюты, остатки кредитов и оценку имущества.
